What this is

High volume, high standards

We tip football selections across the major European leagues and international tournaments. It's a high volume service, typically around 150 to 200 bets a month across a range of markets, but we only post when the research genuinely supports it, so you won't find any filler.

Hybrid models

We find value through a mix of quantitative modelling and qualitative analysis, targeting markets where bookmakers believe they're strong but, in our view, have it wrong. Those edges exist across a wider range of markets than most people assume, and we're constantly developing new models and finding new angles to stay ahead.

Built for this tournament

For the World Cup we've gone further than usual. We've built a full Monte Carlo model of the tournament that simulates every match from our own international ratings, and it sits alongside the qualitative read we bring to every game. It drives the in-tournament selections in the World Cup Pass.

Big bets

We post big bets when we find a good opportunity to compound value at higher odds, not on a fixed schedule but whenever the edge is there. They're well in profit this season, headlined by a 54/1 winner.

Keeping you in the loop

Bets are sent directly to subscribers via Telegram, which is fast and simple and keeps your inbox clear. World Cup Pass holders get every tournament selection the moment it's ready, with a clear price and a minimum acceptable price on each one.

Transparent records

Everything is logged from day one to flat stakes and is independently proofed. We don't inflate a winning month by suddenly upping the stake or quietly shrink it on the losers, so the record you see is the complete and unmanipulated picture.

Getting bets on

A large number of our selections are available in betting shops across multiple UK bookmakers, so you're not reliant on any one firm and our bets are spread around. Most are available at more than one bookie, and we'll sometimes offer an alternative bet from the same angle if you're restricted or simply prefer a different market. Unlike racing, the price you see is almost always the price you'll get, because bookmakers price these markets independently and for most of what we tip there's no liquid exchange market for them to follow.
